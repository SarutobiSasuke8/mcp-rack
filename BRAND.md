# MCP Rack identity

## Idea
The Rack R: an R-shaped chassis with two open server bays. The angled right leg makes the symbol distinguishable from a generic hamburger or server-stack icon. The symbol is original SVG geometry, shared by navigation and social artwork through src/lib/brand.mjs.

## Positioning
Independent MCP tools, curated picks and practical notes. A selection with a named maintainer. Never imply certification, partner status or comprehensive coverage.

## System
- Wordmark: MCP Rack, always two words and this capitalization. Geist at weight 620 in the site.
- Mark: public/brand/mcp-rack-mark.svg; light-on-dark variant beside it.
- Editable lockups: public/brand/mcp-rack-logo.svg and mcp-rack-logo-dark.svg. Text uses Geist with Arial fallback; install Geist when editing/exporting these assets.
- Avatar: public/brand/mcp-rack-icon-512.png.
- Favicon: public/favicon.svg. Apple touch icon: public/apple-touch-icon.png.
- Minimum symbol: 20px. Keep clear space at least one bay height. Do not stretch, outline, rotate or decorate the symbol.
- Graphite #101918; paper #FAF8F4; forest #356958; sage #C5D9CD. Use forest on paper and sage on graphite.
- Geist for UI; Geist Mono for data; Georgia italic only for occasional editorial emphasis.
- Use plain, specific copy. Source and limitation before promotional claims.

## Client marks
OpenAI/ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Grok icons identify footer destinations. Attribution and upstream license are in public/clients. They are not part of MCP Rack branding or a partner strip.
